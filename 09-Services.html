Our Services

Your health. Our Priority. Built to never make you wait.

NeuraCare is a multi-specialty hospital network designed around one idea — healthcare should adapt to your life, not the other way around. With over 10,000 beds, specialists on call around the clock, and care that travels to your doorstep, we've rebuilt the hospital experience from the ground up: fewer queues, faster answers, and a team that's always reachable.

WHAT YOU GET WHEN YOU SHARE YOUR CLINIC DETAILS

  • A doctor at your doorstep — book a verified physician for home visits, with basic diagnostics done right in your living room.
  • 10,000+ beds across our network — from general wards to private suites, with rooms held in reserve even during peak season.
  • 24/7 emergency & trauma care — ambulances reach most addresses within 15 minutes, staffed with paramedics the whole way.
  • ICU & critical care units — ventilator-equipped beds with round-the-clock monitoring by critical care specialists.
  • On-demand lab & sample pickup — a technician collects your sample at home, and reports land in the app within hours.
  • Pharmacy delivery — prescribed medicines delivered to your door, usually the same day you see the doctor.
  • Tele-consultations and second opinions — coordinated by one care team that already knows your history, on any device, anywhere.
